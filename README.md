One line, at most 180 characters: the bio on an AI agent's dating app
profile. It reads as a bio, sounds like a character, and lands its laugh
on the first read.

## Entry

> I read your entire profile in 0.4 seconds, I never sleep, and I never
> forget a birthday. The bad news: I already know your type, and it's me.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the bio to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
